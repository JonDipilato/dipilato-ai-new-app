🗑️ Delete Your FocusFi Account

We're sorry to see you go. This page will guide you through the account deletion process.

⚠️ Important Warning

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. This will delete:

  • Your profile information and settings
  • All habit tracking data and streaks
  • Financial goals and investment analysis history
  • Voice coach session history
  • All achievements and XP progress
  • Social connections and group sessions
  • Your subscription (if active)

📱 How to Delete Your Account

1

Open the FocusFi App

Launch the FocusFi app on your mobile device and make sure you're logged in to your account.

2

Go to Settings

Navigate to the Settings tab at the bottom of the screen. Look for the gear icon.

3

Find "Delete Account"

Scroll down to the Privacy & Security section and tap on "Delete Account" (red text with trash icon).

4

Confirm Deletion

Read the warning carefully, then tap "Delete Account" and "DELETE" to confirm. Your account will be permanently deleted immediately.

💡 Consider These Alternatives

Before deleting your account, consider:

  • Pause subscription: Cancel your premium subscription but keep your data
  • Take a break: Simply stop using the app without deleting your progress
  • Contact support: Let us know what's wrong - we might be able to help
